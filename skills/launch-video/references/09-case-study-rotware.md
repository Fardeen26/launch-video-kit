# 09 · Case study: the Rotware launch film

Rotware (rotware.app) is a meme editor: templates, image search, AI image edits, captions, GIF and video tools.
Mascot: ROT-1, a comic-style muscle chad. Theme: Windows 95. Tagline: "the only software you need for memes".
Channels: X, Product Hunt, Reddit, Instagram. Final film: 44s, 1080p + 4K + 9:16.

## Lessons in one list

1. A joke with no reason to be in the video can't be saved by better execution.
2. Real footage + real product beats generated art every time for this audience.
3. Music and SFX can fully replace voiceover; the user judged the music by ear, the agent only measured it.
4. Muted-first: the first meme must land by ~2s.
5. After the drop, only change lengths by whole bars.
6. The hero product moment must be readable at phone size.
7. Scene-specific mascot poses, not one reused image.
8. Captions must look like the meme formats people actually post.
9. Keep the opening clip short (4s, not 9s), even when the full moment is great.
10. Check the disk before 4K renders.
