# 01 · Creative direction

## The spine

> The internet takes a small moment from something much bigger and turns it into the thing everybody remembers.
> Here is the place to make yours.

That one idea gives the video a reason to exist, connects the opening to the product, and lets the demo be a story
instead of a feature carousel. Keep it; swap the examples.

## Choosing the opening moments

Rules (from the user's brief, all held up):
- The meme must **genuinely come from the shown moment**. Verify on Know Your Meme. "And I took that personally"
  (Michael Jordan, *The Last Dance*) was dropped: Jordan never says it, and nobody can identify the scene.
- Understandable in ~2 seconds without context. Prefer visual + one line.
- Recognisable to the target audience. Gen-Z/internet-native viewers: Uncut Gems "This is how I win" beat Allen
  Iverson "practice" because more of them know it. Ask the user which moments THEIR audience knows.
- No sexual content, graphic violence, or contentious political claims.
- One of them should connect to your hero meme (Cybertruck window -> "me after the live demo").

What we used: Steve Ballmer "Developers! Developers!" (full chant, then "PRODUCT MANAGERS WHEN / SOMETHING BREAKS AT
3 AM"), Uncut Gems "This is how I win" (user-supplied meme image), Cybertruck window smash ("IT DIDN'T GO THROUGH").

## The hero meme

One meme runs through the whole product section so the viewer watches it being made. It must:
1. Be about something the audience lives (founders and creators: live demos going wrong).
2. Come from or echo the opening (same Cybertruck footage the opening just showed).
3. Be funny as a still with no explanation. If the rough isn't funny, more rendering won't fix it.
4. Need an AI edit that is **obvious at phone size** (whole truck -> giant cracked "illegal operation" Win95 error).
5. Exist in three formats: image, video, GIF.

Rejected attempts and why:
- v1 "me skipping the tutorial / me 30 seconds later" as a polished AI illustration of the mascot in a game arena:
  "cringe, boomer, slop". Generic joke, glossy AI look, no connection to the product or audience.
- v2 same joke, meme-native execution (real art, photo-pasted plastic chair, Win95 TUTORIAL.EXE): "I don't get the
  purpose of it". Execution can't save a joke with no reason to be there.
- v3 Cybertruck live demo meme: approved instantly. Real footage, real stakes, self-aware (this IS a launch demo).

## Tone (study Memelord / Jester launches)

- Commit 100% to the bit (epic production for a trivial thing).
- Mascots are physical characters in a real world, not logos.
- Competitors and the industry become characters (the OpenAI logo as a blob).
- Wordless skits beat explained jokes.
- Avoid: dad jokes, "the future of content is here", feature carousels with a random joke per tool, long mascot intros,
  fake engagement, expensive VFX hiding an unclear demo.

## Visual identity

Pick ONE world and stay in it. Rotware: Windows 95 (grey bevelled windows, navy title bars, pixel cursor, hills
wallpaper). The template's `theme` swaps colours/wallpaper/fonts; keep the window chrome square and bevelled.
Keep footage clean: no heavy VHS filter over everything.

## Deliverables

- 16:9 1080p master (X, Reddit, Product Hunt via unlisted YouTube link) + 4K master.
- 9:16 cut (Instagram Reels, TikTok, Shorts) via `FilmVertical`.
